## Question

Prerequisite: [What battlefield layout creates competing demands on commander position?](07-battlefield-layout.md).

For the agreed mirrored two-route battlefield, what starting forces, reinforcement group sizes and cadence, and broad battlefield scale create competing fights without overwhelming the player?

Decide where the commander and initial soldiers begin, how successive reinforcement groups activate the routes, and what qualitative relationship between route separation, influence radius, and travel time makes relocation matter. Choose a prototype starting configuration rather than a final balance prescription; exact combat statistics and numerical tuning can remain adjustable.

Test whether both routes can remain relevant, whether fresh reinforcements can contest a threatened home area, and whether the commander can react to a new threat without trivially supporting both fights. Account for the roughly ten-minute battle target, automatic enemy stance switching, and the shared cross-passage.
