## Question

With the agreed mirrored two-route battle, readable feedback, starting forces, and adjustable tuning, what is the smallest representative playable encounter and evaluation procedure that can establish whether commander positioning creates understandable, worthwhile choices?

Decide whether the normal starting battle is sufficient or whether evaluation needs repeatable scenario starts. Specify how to exercise supporting and leaving a fight, switching routes, returning to contest a threatened base with friendly soldiers, escaping to heal, and prolonged Defend. Choose observable evidence for meaningful relocation versus dominant camping, readable danger, recoverable setbacks, and excessive accumulation. Distinguish evidence about the core-loop design from symptoms that should first prompt numerical tuning.

Define the necessary evaluation duration, replay or reset conditions, and lightweight observations to record, including how to judge the roughly ten-minute pacing target. This ticket resolves the evaluation plan; building or running the playable prototype is beyond this map's planning destination.

## Answer

The user confirmed the following initial evaluation plan. It defines observations for the future playable prototype; building and running that prototype remain beyond this planning map.

### Runs and repeatability

Begin with three fresh normal battles using the agreed six-soldier-per-side start: active repositioning, deliberately camping one useful position, and prolonged Defend. Keep layout, starting forces, reinforcement sequence, and tuning identical across comparisons. Do not reset mid-run to erase mistakes.

Observe each run for about ten minutes unless victory or defeat ends it earlier. Allow the Defend run up to fifteen minutes when needed to expose accumulation. If no normal battle finishes, extend a normal run to fifteen minutes and record whether it finishes or remains unresolved. Record actual completion times; an unfinished observation window leaves the roughly ten-minute pacing target unverified rather than establishing success or failure.

When tuning changes, record the change and replay affected comparisons from the same starting conditions. Targeted scenarios must also have repeatable starting states and retain the normal battle rules.

### Required situation coverage

Evaluation remains incomplete until the following have been observed:

- Supporting a fight long enough to build stationary influence, then leaving it and observing the cost.
- Switching routes to address a competing need.
- Responding to a threatened player base while friendly soldiers can still contest capture.
- Escaping commander danger and recovering health.
- Prolonged Defend and its effect on soldier accumulation and readability.

Use the normal battle as the primary test. Add repeatable targeted starts only for coverage ordinary play does not provide. For home defense, compare timely and delayed responses from the same starting situation. A delayed loss is acceptable; timely action should offer a plausible save through friendly defenders. Commander arrival alone cannot contest capture.

### Player understanding and observations

Give a brief introduction to controls, commander influence, and the battle objective. Before explaining particular events, ask what the player noticed, why they moved or stayed, and what they expected. Compare their explanation with actual events. Pause may be used for discussion; orders remain prohibited while paused.

Keep records lightweight:

- Battle outcome and elapsed time, distinguishing completed battles from observation-window stops.
- Important relocations: intended benefit, cost of leaving, and observed result.
- Whether home danger was noticed early enough to respond with friendly defenders.
- Whether commander danger was understandable and escape allowed healing.
- Whether crowds became difficult to read, especially repeated local fights above roughly eight soldiers per side.

### Provisional verdict and tuning

Judge meaningful positioning by understandable trade-offs and observed consequences, not by requiring relocation to always outperform staying. Supporting one fight should help it; leaving should sacrifice some advantage; moving should sometimes address a competing need. Staying can be locally correct. Concern arises when one camping position reliably handles the whole battle or relocation rarely changes anything useful.

Summarize each evaluation area as **promising**, **needs tuning**, or **needs a design revisit**, supported by concrete observations. Inspect the agreed numerical tuning values first. Reopen behavioral rules when problems persist across reasonable adjustments or reveal a contradiction in the design. The initial evaluation provides provisional evidence, not a strict pass/fail score or a guaranteed ten-minute outcome.
