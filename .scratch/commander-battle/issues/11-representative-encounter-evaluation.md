# What representative encounter and observations will test the commander-positioning core loop?

Map: [Find the design for a commander-positioning battle](../map.md)
Labels: wayfinder:grilling
Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 09, 10

## Question

With the agreed mirrored two-route battle, readable feedback, starting forces, and adjustable tuning, what is the smallest representative playable encounter and evaluation procedure that can establish whether commander positioning creates understandable, worthwhile choices?

Decide whether the normal starting battle is sufficient or whether evaluation needs repeatable scenario starts. Specify how to exercise supporting and leaving a fight, switching routes, returning to contest a threatened base with friendly soldiers, escaping to heal, and prolonged Defend. Choose observable evidence for meaningful relocation versus dominant camping, readable danger, recoverable setbacks, and excessive accumulation. Distinguish evidence about the core-loop design from symptoms that should first prompt numerical tuning.

Define the necessary evaluation duration, replay or reset conditions, and lightweight observations to record, including how to judge the roughly ten-minute pacing target. This ticket resolves the evaluation plan; building or running the playable prototype is beyond this map's planning destination.
